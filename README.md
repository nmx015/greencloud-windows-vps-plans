## The fine print that actually affects your decision

**Support is unmanaged, full stop.** GreenCloud's own pages state: "Our services are unmanaged. We take care of the physical server and its uptime only. The Managed line doesn't publish an itemized list of what "managed" adds, so if you're paying $40+/mo partly for hands-on software help, ask their 24/7 ticket system what's included before ordering. Response times are reportedly fast (official pages cite ~9 minutes average), so getting a straight answer shouldn't take long.

**The 7-day refund is narrow.** It applies to new customers, on their first VPS only, and special-discount plans are excluded. If you want a safety net for your first month, that's your window — and it's another reason to start with a monthly-billed Normal plan rather than a prepaid quarterly one.

**Provisioning and verification.** Servers usually come online within 15 minutes, occasionally up to 2 hours. Inaccurate billing details can trip the anti-fraud system and delay things. New customers paying by credit/debit card or PayPal go through a one-time verification — once per payment method, not per order.

**Upgrades have a ceiling.** RAM, CPU cores, and SSD can be added à la carte at $5 per unit (per 1GB RAM, per core, per 20GB SSD — 10GB on Forex plans), but only up to the same amount as the original config. A 4GB plan maxes out at 8GB total. If you expect to outgrow that, start with a bigger plan instead of stacking add-ons.

**The admin access itself has no gotchas.** Some hosts throttle what you can do with "admin" rights; GreenCloud explicitly permits traffic exchange tools like Hitleap and Jingling on the Budget line, and names SEO automation suites on the SEO line. For anyone searching "windows vps with admin access" specifically to run automation, that written permission is worth more than a spec-sheet difference.
